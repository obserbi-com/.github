<h1 align="center">ObserBI</h1>

<p align="center"><strong>Know what your numbers will do — before they do it.</strong></p>

ObserBI is a sovereign AI CFO in a box, designed to plug into the ERP you already run, close the control loop over finance, operations and strategy, and keep the models on your own hardware.

## How it works

1. **Connect.** Read-only connectors into your ERP, banking, HR and project systems. Nothing is migrated or rewritten.
2. **Close the loop.** Loop 1 keeps the operating envelope — cash, margin, utilisation, DSO — under continuous control. Loop 2 watches loop 1 and proposes new rules when reality moves; a person approves them.
3. **Act.** A daily briefing, a ranked list of decisions and the reasoning behind each one, in your language, on your hardware.

## What is inside the box

- Continuous controlling: budget, forecast and actuals reconciled every night, with the variance explained.
- Sovereign by construction: local models, so ledger lines and customer names stay inside your network.
- Outside signal: supporting agents for OSINT, GEOINT and market intelligence.
- Decisions that are logged, explainable and auditable, with EU rules on AI in mind from the start.
